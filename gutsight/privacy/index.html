Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Gutsight is a gut-health diary. It’s built so that your information stays on your iPhone. We don’t collect it, see it or sell it.

The short version

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • Everything you log is stored only on your device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party data collection.
  • The app’s only network traffic is with Apple’s App Store, to show the price of Full Access and confirm purchases. There are no Gutsight servers.

What the app stores, and where

When you use Gutsight, it saves what you enter: meals and drinks, symptoms, bowel movements, daily check-ins (gut score, stress, sleep, water and, if you turn it on, period days), foods you add, FODMAP plan progress and challenge results, and your settings.

This information is kept in the app’s private storage on your device. It’s never sent to us or to anyone else.

Like other apps’ data, it can be included in the device backups you make with iCloud or a computer. Those backups are controlled by your Apple account and settings, not by Gutsight.

Purchases

Full Access is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment, and the app asks the App Store on your iPhone whether your Apple Account owns Full Access. We don’t receive your name, email address or payment details. Purchases are covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

App lock

If you turn on the lock, iOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. Gutsight only learns whether the check passed. It never sees or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled and delivered by your iPhone. Nothing about them is sent over the internet.

Reports, exports and backups

You can create a PDF report, export spreadsheets (CSV) or save a backup file. These are only created when you ask, and you choose where they go, such as Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or an email to your doctor. Once a file leaves the app, it’s handled by the service you send it to.

Health information

Gutsight doesn’t share your health information with anyone and never uses it for advertising or marketing. The app doesn’t use HealthKit.

Deleting your data

Settings › Erase All Data removes everything from the app. Deleting the app also deletes its data from your device.

Children

Gutsight isn’t directed at children under 13, and it doesn’t knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because the app collects no data, changes would mostly concern new features.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See Gutsight support.

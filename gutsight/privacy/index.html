Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Gutsight is a gut-health diary. It’s built so that your information stays on your iPhone. We don’t collect it, see it or sell it.

The short version

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • Everything you log is stored only on your device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party data collection.
  • The AI features run on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence. Your meals, photos and symptoms aren’t sent to us or to an AI service.
  • The app’s only network traffic is with Apple’s App Store, to show Gutsight Pro’s prices and confirm purchases, and with Open Food Facts when you scan a barcode, to look up that product. There are no Gutsight servers.

What the app stores, and where

When you use Gutsight, it saves what you enter: meals and drinks, symptoms, bowel movements, daily check-ins (gut score, stress, sleep, water and, if you turn it on, period days), foods you add, FODMAP plan progress and challenge results, and your settings.

This information is kept in the app’s private storage on your device. It’s never sent to us or to anyone else.

Like other apps’ data, it can be included in the device backups you make with iCloud or a computer. Those backups are controlled by your Apple account and settings, not by Gutsight.

AI features and photos

Snap a Meal, Scan a Menu, Fix a Recipe’s photo reading, Ask Gutsight and the weekly summary use Apple’s on-device language model (Apple Intelligence). The photo you take or choose, the questions you ask, and the parts of your log used to answer them or write the summary, are processed on your iPhone. They aren’t sent to Gutsight, Apple or any other AI service, and Gutsight doesn’t keep the photos after reading them. The camera and photo library are used only when you choose these features, and iOS asks your permission first.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode, or type its number, Gutsight sends that number to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org), a free, open food database run by a non-profit, to get the product’s name and ingredients. Only the barcode number is sent, with no account, name or anything from your log. Like any website, Open Food Facts receives your device’s IP address with the request; its own privacy policy covers that. The answer is saved on your iPhone so the same product works offline next time. The camera reads the barcode on your iPhone; no images are sent.

Purchases

Gutsight Pro is bought through Apple, as a subscription or a one-time purchase. Apple handles the payment and renewals, and the app asks the App Store on your iPhone whether your Apple Account has Gutsight Pro. We don’t receive your name, email address or payment details. Purchases are covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

App lock

If you turn on the lock, iOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. Gutsight only learns whether the check passed. It never sees or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled and delivered by your iPhone. Nothing about them is sent over the internet.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in Settings, Gutsight adds the symptoms, loose stools and water you log to the Health app, and keeps them in step when you edit or delete an entry. Gutsight never reads your other Health data. Health data is never used for advertising and is stored by Apple, not by us. You can turn this off in Settings, or in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

Reports, exports and backups

You can create a PDF report, export spreadsheets (CSV) or save a backup file. These are only created when you ask, and you choose where they go, such as Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or an email to your doctor. Once a file leaves the app, it’s handled by the service you send it to.

Gutsight also saves an automatic backup once a day, keeping the last 14. If you use iCloud Drive, they go to a Gutsight folder in your own iCloud Drive, stored by Apple under your Apple account; we can’t see them. Otherwise they’re kept on your iPhone. You can turn automatic backups off in Settings, and delete them in the Files app.

Health information

Gutsight doesn’t share your health information with anyone and never uses it for advertising or marketing. The app doesn’t use HealthKit.

Deleting your data

Settings › Erase All Data removes everything from the app. Deleting the app also deletes its data from your device.

Children

Gutsight isn’t directed at children under 13, and it doesn’t knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because the app collects no data, changes would mostly concern new features.

Questions

How does Gutsight find my triggers?

For each food, it compares how often symptoms started within a few hours of eating it with how often they followed your other meals. The window is 8 hours unless you change it in Settings › Trigger finder. It needs about 10 meals and 3 symptoms to start, and patterns firm up the longer you log. They’re patterns from your own diary, not proof.

Why isn’t a food I suspect showing up?

A pattern needs several meals with the food and several without it. Foods eaten together share the blame, so one that only ever appears next to a stronger trigger is explained away. In Insights, pick a single symptom, such as heartburn or loose stools, to see its own triggers.

How do I move my diary to a new iPhone?

On the old iPhone, go to Settings › Back Up All Data and save the file to iCloud Drive with “Save to Files”. On the new one, install Gutsight and choose Settings › Restore from Backup.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Erase All Data removes everything. Deleting the app does too. Your diary isn’t stored anywhere else, apart from backups you made yourself.

My reminders don’t appear.

Check that notifications are allowed in the iPhone Settings app › Notifications › Gutsight, then turn the reminders on again in Gutsight’s Settings.

How does the app lock work?

Turn it on in Settings › Privacy. Gutsight then asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode when you open it. iOS does the check, and if Face ID fails it offers your passcode.

Is Gutsight medical advice?

No. It’s for tracking and education, and it doesn’t diagnose or treat any condition. See a doctor about ongoing or severe symptoms, and before big changes to your diet. The app’s “When to See a Doctor” guide lists the warning signs that need prompt care.

Which iPhones can use the AI features?

Snap a Meal, Scan a Menu and the weekly summary use Apple Intelligence, on iPhone 15 Pro or newer with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Photos need iOS 27. Everything else in Gutsight works on any supported iPhone.

What’s free, and what’s in Gutsight Pro?

Logging, the FODMAP check on every meal, the food guide, condition guides, label checks, meal ideas, insights, reminders, the app lock and exports are free, with no time limit. Gutsight Pro adds the trigger finder’s results, meal photos, menu scans, Ask Gutsight, the weekly summary, the meal planner, guided reintroduction and the PDF report for your doctor. It’s $34.99 a year with the first week free, $6.99 a month, or $79.99 once for life. Cancel a subscription any time in your Apple Account settings.

How do I get Gutsight Pro back on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Settings › Restore Purchases in Gutsight. Your diary moves separately, with a backup (see above).

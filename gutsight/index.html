Gutsight

IBS & FODMAP diary for iPhone

Find the foods behind your bloating, cramps and urgency. Log meals in your own words, follow the low-FODMAP plan step by step, and keep it all private. One price, no subscription.

What it does

  • Log in seconds. Type a meal the way you’d say it. Gutsight picks out the ingredients and their FODMAPs, and suggests swaps.
  • Find your triggers. It compares how you felt after each food with how you felt after everything else, and says how strong the evidence is.
  • Follow the low-FODMAP plan. Elimination with meal ideas, then reintroduction one FODMAP group at a time.
  • Check a label. Spot hidden FODMAPs like inulin, honey and onion powder in any ingredient list.
  • Share with your doctor. A PDF report of your symptoms, bowel movements and likely triggers.
  • Private by design. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your diary stays on your iPhone.

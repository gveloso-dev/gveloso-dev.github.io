IBS & FODMAP tracker for iPhone

Bloating, cramps, urgency, and no idea why? Log meals in your own words, and Gutsight shows which foods line up with your symptoms. Then follow the low-FODMAP plan to learn what you can eat again.

What it does

  • Log in seconds. Type a meal the way you’d say it, like “pasta with garlic and an oat milk latte”. Gutsight picks out the ingredients and their FODMAPs, and suggests swaps.
  • Find your triggers. It compares how you felt after each food with how you felt after everything else, says how strong the evidence is, and looks at one symptom at a time if you like.
  • Follow the low-FODMAP plan. Elimination with meal ideas, then reintroduction one FODMAP group at a time, in rising doses.
  • Check a label. Spot hidden FODMAPs like inulin, honey and onion powder in any ingredient list.
  • Share with your doctor. A PDF report of your symptoms, bowel movements and likely triggers.
  • Log from the Home Screen. Press and hold the icon to log food, a symptom or a stool.
  • Made for everyone. Works with VoiceOver, the largest text sizes and Dark Mode.

Gutsight

IBS & FODMAP tracker for iPhone

Bloating, heartburn, cramps, and no idea why? Snap or type your meals, and Gutsight shows which foods line up with your symptoms, guides you with advice from clinical guidelines, and tells you which dishes on a menu suit you.

Coming soon to the App Store

What it does

  • Log in seconds. Type a meal the way you’d say it, or snap a photo. Gutsight picks out the ingredients, including hidden ones like garlic in a sauce, notes their FODMAPs and suggests swaps.
  • Find your triggers. It compares how you felt after each food with how you felt after everything else, says how strong the evidence is, and looks at one symptom at a time if you like.
  • Scan a menu. Photograph a restaurant menu and see good picks, dishes to go easy on and ones likely to bother you, based on your own triggers.
  • Guides for your condition. IBS, bloating, constipation, reflux, gastritis and H. pylori, indigestion, lactose intolerance, celiac disease, Crohn’s and colitis, microscopic colitis, bile acid diarrhea, pancreatic insufficiency, eosinophilic esophagitis, diverticular disease, gastroparesis and SIBO, each with what helps, rated by strength of evidence.
  • Every source cited. The Science screen lists the guidelines and studies behind every feature, what they found and how Gutsight uses them.
  • Follow the low-FODMAP plan. Elimination with meal ideas, then reintroduction one FODMAP group at a time, in rising doses.
  • Check a label. Spot hidden FODMAPs, and gluten if you need to avoid it, in any ingredient list.
  • Log from your Home Screen. Widgets show today’s log and open straight to logging a meal, a symptom or a bowel movement.
  • Share with your doctor. A PDF report of your symptoms, bowel movements and likely triggers.
  • Made for everyone. Works with VoiceOver, the largest text sizes and Dark Mode.
